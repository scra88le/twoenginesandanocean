The engine's product is motion. This is the kinetic energy of the whole air column in January, with the mean flow at 250 hPa, about 10 km up. The jet streams are strongest in the winter hemisphere, where the equator-to-pole temperature difference is largest: a bigger difference gives the engine more to work with. Step to July and the northern jets weaken. Friction near the ground alone turns about **2.4 W m⁻²** of this motion back into heat, about 1 % of the sunlight absorbed.
