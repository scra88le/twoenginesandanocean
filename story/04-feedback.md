Convection lifts heat from the surface, and in doing so cools the surface and warms the air above it, which weakens the temperature difference that drives it. Kleidon and Renner's single-column model finds the heat flux that gives the most power: half the absorbed sunlight.

This map compares the real flux of evaporation plus sensible heat with that ideal, over **land only**. Near 1 the land works close to maximum power. Far from 1 the simple model fits poorly: it ignores heat storage and lateral transport.
