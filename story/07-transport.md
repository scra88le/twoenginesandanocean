Averaged over a year, the atmosphere exports energy from the tropics (red) and imports it at high latitudes (blue). The arrows show the part of the flux that runs from sources to sinks, and they point poleward. Across 35° latitude, air and ocean together carry about **5 petawatts**: roughly 4 by the air and 1 by the sea. That is more than 200 times humanity's energy use. Switch to Today and this map fills with storms: on a single day, weather systems store and release far more energy than this steady flow.
