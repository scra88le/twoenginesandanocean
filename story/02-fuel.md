Engine one starts at the ground. The surface absorbs about **164 W m⁻²** on average, two thirds of the sunlight the planet takes up: over 200 W m⁻² in the tropics and under 40 near the poles. The surface has to get rid of that heat. Some leaves as infrared radiation. The rest is carried up by rising air and evaporating water. That second route is convection, and convection is a heat engine: it turns part of the heat it carries into motion.
