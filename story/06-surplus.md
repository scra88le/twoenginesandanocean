Engine two runs between the tropics and the poles. The chart averages the map around each latitude. Absorbed sunlight (red) peaks at the equator, while outgoing infrared (blue) is much flatter, because heat is carried away from where it arrives. Kleidon's two-box model splits Earth at 30° and asks which transport gives the most power: about **31 W m⁻²** per square metre of each half, against **41 W m⁻²** in ERA5. The panel shows the same calculation for the data.
