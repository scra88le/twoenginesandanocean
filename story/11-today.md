Everything so far has been the average engine. This is the engine running on the date in the time bar: that day's column kinetic energy and winds at 100 m. Day to day, the engine is a weather map. Storms build and release energy far faster than the steady flows behind them, which is why the concepts need long averages to show. Switch to **Today vs normal** to see how this day differs from the 1991–2020 average for its month.
