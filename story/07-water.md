Water is the engine's working fluid. It evaporates at the surface, is carried upward, and releases its heat where it condenses and falls as rain. Rainfall is shown here on a logarithmic scale, from a trace to the heaviest rates in the tropics.

The globe averages about **3 mm per day**. Lifting that water against gravity takes power too: the *Power lifting water* layer, marked indicative because it assumes a fixed lift height, estimates how much.
