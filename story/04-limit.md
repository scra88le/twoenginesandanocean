Convection undermines itself. The more heat it lifts, the more it cools the surface and warms the air, shrinking the temperature difference that drives it. Kleidon and Renner showed that power peaks when the flux carries about **half the sunlight absorbed at the surface**. This map divides the real flux of evaporation plus sensible heat by that optimum. Values near 1 suggest the land works close to its limit. The theory is meant for long-term averages like this one, not single days.
