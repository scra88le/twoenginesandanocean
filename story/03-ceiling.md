No engine can turn all of its heat into work. The ceiling, the Carnot limit, depends only on the hot and cold sides: **η = (T_s − T_r) / T_s**. The hot side is the surface. The cold side is Earth's radiative temperature, about **256 K (−17 °C)**: the temperature at which the planet sheds the sunlight it absorbs. The ceiling is highest over hot tropical land and falls to zero over polar ice, where the surface is colder than that. Averaged over the globe it is about 11 %.
