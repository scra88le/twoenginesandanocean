title: Ceiling

No heat engine can turn all its heat into work. The ceiling depends only on the temperatures of its hot and cold sides, and here those are the surface and the emission temperature: **η = (T_s − T_e) / T_s**.

Averaged over the globe it is about 12 %, higher over hot land and lower towards the poles, and hatched where it falls to zero. This is a ceiling, not a measurement of what the atmosphere achieves: the real engine converts only a small share of it into wind.
