The ocean is the system's storage. This map shows the net heat flowing into the surface in July. The summer-hemisphere ocean absorbs heat (red) and the winter-hemisphere ocean gives it back (blue): between 20° and 60° the northern ocean takes up about **110 W m⁻²** and the southern ocean loses about as much. Because water holds so much heat, the ocean smooths the seasons. Step through the months to watch the charge move with the Sun. Over land the flow nearly balances across the year.
