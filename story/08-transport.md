The engine also moves energy. This map shows the **divergence of the total energy flux**, smoothed over about 100 km: red where energy is exported, blue where it piles up.

The arrows show the *divergent* part of the flux, pointing from sources towards sinks, so they carry the energy the engine really moves, mostly towards the poles. The part of the flow that only circulates, without delivering any energy, is left out. Arrow length is proportional to the flux.
