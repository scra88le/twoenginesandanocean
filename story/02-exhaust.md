Heat leaves the Earth as infrared radiation. Seen from space, what leaves matches a surface at the **effective emission temperature**, T_e = (OLR / σ)^¼.

On average T_e is about **255 K (−18 °C)**, far colder than the ground, because most of the radiation escapes from high in the atmosphere. It is coldest above tall storm clouds and warmest over clear, hot deserts. T_e is the engine's cold side: the lower it is compared with the surface, the more work the engine can do.
