The engine runs on sunlight. Over a year the Earth absorbs about **240 W m⁻²** at the top of the atmosphere and radiates about the same back to space, so the planet stays near balance.

This map shows one day's difference: red where the Earth gains energy, mostly the sunlit tropics and the summer hemisphere, and blue where it loses energy, mostly the winter hemisphere. Energy gained in one place and lost in another is what drives the circulation of the atmosphere.
