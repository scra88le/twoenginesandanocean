The engine's output is motion. Convection creates the atmosphere's kinetic energy, and most of it is lost to friction near the ground, shown here as **boundary-layer dissipation** on a logarithmic scale.

The white streaks are the 100 m wind. Dissipation is strongest under storms and over the windiest oceans. Averaged over the globe it is only about **2 W m⁻²**, under 1 % of the sunlight absorbed: the atmosphere is an inefficient engine that does a great deal of moving.
